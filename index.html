Instructions

El Pollo Loco is a fast-paced jump-and-run adventure game where you play as Pepe, a brave little hero on a mission to defeat crazy chickens and collect treasures. Players explore a colorful Mexican-inspired world filled with obstacles, coins, and surprises.

Use the left and right arrow keys to move Pepe, the up arrow key to jump, and key d to throw bottles.

Your goal is to survive and defeat the powerful Endboss.
