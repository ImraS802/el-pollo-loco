El Pollo Loco

Instructions

El Pollo Loco is a fast-paced jump-and-run adventure game where you play as Pepe, a brave little hero on a mission to defeat crazy chickens and collect treasures. Players explore a colorful Mexican-inspired world filled with obstacles, coins, and surprises.
Use the left and right arrow keys to move Pepe in the corresponding direction, the up arrow key to jump, and press the key d to throw bottles at enemies.
Your goal is to survive, collect as many items as possible, and defeat the powerful Endboss Chicken to restore peace to the chaotic chicken world.
With vibrant graphics, smooth controls, and fun sound effects, El Pollo Loco delivers a classic platforming experience full of action and humor.
